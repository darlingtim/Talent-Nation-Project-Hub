# Mentors, Heads of Campus and portfolios

Related issues: #7, #9, #12

- Mentors and Heads upload **portfolios** (picture + links), not projects.
- Portfolios are kept out of Spotlight and Explore, and are public on profiles and the Coding Mentors page.
- The database sets is_portfolio automatically for anyone who is a mentor or Head.
- The Coding Mentors page lists Heads of Campus first, then Coding Mentors, with a campus filter and search.
- The Fellows page lists fellows only.
